# Changelog

## v1.0 Pre-Release 2

**Added**
- Gyro aiming in first person. Turn it on in Enhancements > Camera > Gyro Aiming.
- A loading screen with a progress bar.

**Improved**
- Much less stutter when changing maps and during the title intro.
- Menus use the screen better, and long labels wrap instead of being cut off.
- The X button on popup windows is bigger and easier to press.

**Fixed**
- The boot logo now shows the 2Ship logo.
- The Check Tracker's color pickers are no longer misplaced.
- The Audio Editor has a clear line under its category tabs.

**Updating:** replace the files in `/switch/2s2h/` and keep your `mm.o2r`. The
first launch takes longer while it prepares shaders.

## v1.0 Pre-Release

- Fixed missing or broken music and sound.
- Fixed the crash during the title sequence.
